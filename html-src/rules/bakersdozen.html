Baker's Dozen

Baker's Dozen · 1 deck · No redeal

Object

Move all cards to the foundations.

Rules

Cards are dealt to thirteen piles of four cards each. All Kings are placed at the bottom of a pile during the initial dealing.

The piles build down by rank regardless of suit. Only one card can be moved at a time. Empty piles cannot be filled.

The foundations are built up by same suit from ace to king. The game is won if all cards are moved to the foundations.
