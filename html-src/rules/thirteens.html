Thirteens

Pairing · 1 deck · No redeal

Object

Discard all the cards.

Rules

Ten cards are dealt to the tableau.

The object is to use up all the cards from the tableau by discarding pairs of cards that total up to 13, and the cards are then replaced from the talon. Kings can be removed on their own.

You win when the tableau piles are all gone.

Notes

Autodrop is disabled for this game.
