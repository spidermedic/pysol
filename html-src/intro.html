Introduction

"Why yet another solitaire game?" you may ask. The answer is simple...

PySol Lite highlights

  • currently supports more than 250 distinct solitaire games
  • based upon an extensible solitaire engine
  • lots of classic games like Forty Thieves, FreeCell, Klondike and Spider
  • special games like Cribbage and Poker, and Mahjongg tile games
  • modern look and feel
  • multiple cardsets and background colors
  • unlimited undo & redo
  • a bookmark
  • load & save games
  • a hint system
  • demo games
  • support for user written plug-ins
  • sound samples
  • integrated HTML help browser
  • lots of documentation
  • portable across Windows, Mac, and Linux
  • completely ad-free, now and forever
  • written in 100% pure Python
