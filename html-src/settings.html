Settings

All of PySol Lite's settings are in one window. On a Mac choose PySol Lite / Settings (Cmd-comma); on Windows and Linux choose Options / Settings (Ctrl-comma). Changes take effect at once, and you can leave the window open while you play. The window has four tabs.

General

Click to move cards moves cards by clicking a card and then clicking where it should go, instead of dragging. Confirm before quitting makes the app ask before it throws away a game you have not finished, for example when you start a new game or quit. Sound turns the game's sound effects on and off; to change how loud they are, use your device's volume control. Animation speed is Off, Slow, Medium or Fast.

Assist

Automatic play lets certain moves play themselves: auto face-up flips exposed cards face-up, auto deal deals cards from the talon when the waste is empty, and auto drop moves cards to the foundations when possible (even when it is off, auto drop can still be triggered by hand). Quick play enables the right mouse button shortcut. Options that do not apply to the current game are grayed out. For more detail on auto drop, see Assist Options.

Shade legal moves highlights the stacks that can accept the cards you are moving.

The Specific games options apply to single kinds of games, such as dealing all cards in Accordion type games or showing removed tiles in Mahjongg games. More info on these can be found with the rules of said games.

Appearance

Deck opens the list of decks (cardsets). Different decks are available for the different kinds of games. The card Back buttons next to it pick a different design for the back of the cards. You can also create custom cardsets from your own images - see cardset customization for more info on how.

Background color is the color behind the cards. Spread stacks spreads the stacks evenly across the window.

The cards are scaled automatically to fit the window, so resize the window to make them larger or smaller. Moving cards always show a shadow under them. The window size and position are remembered separately for each game. The fonts follow your system fonts, and the text on the table switches between white and black to suit the background color.

Toolbar

Choose which side of the window the toolbar is on, the size of its icons, whether the buttons show icons, text or both, and which buttons are visible. To show or hide the toolbar or the status bar, use the View menu, which also has Full screen (F11, or the Enter Full Screen item macOS adds).

Where Settings are Stored

PySol Lite settings are stored in a configuration file, options.dat or options.cfg depending on your environment - this file is located in the user folder.

You may back up this file, but editing it manually is not recommended and can break your configuration.
